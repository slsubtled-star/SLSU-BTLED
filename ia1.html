Circuit building

Circuit building on a breadboard is the process of assembling a simple electronic circuit using components such as resistors, LEDs, and connecting wires. The image demonstrates how these parts are arranged and connected based on a circuit diagram to form a working electrical system. This activity allows students to experiment with different connections without permanently attaching the components. Building circuits on a breadboard helps learners understand how electricity flows and how basic electronic devices function.

Gearbox disassembly

Gearbox disassembly is the process of taking apart a transmission unit to examine its internal components. The image demonstrates how students use basic hand tools to carefully remove parts such as gears, shafts, bearings, and synchronizers from the transmission housing. This activity allows learners to observe the condition of the components, count gear teeth for ratio analysis, and understand how the parts work together inside the mechanism. Gearbox disassembly is commonly used in industrial arts and vocational training to develop knowledge in automotive repair, machinery maintenance, and gear train principles.

Soldering

Soldering is the process of joining metal wires together using a soldering iron and melted solder. The image demonstrates how the heated tip of the soldering iron melts the solder, allowing it to bond copper wires and other metal components securely. This technique requires careful handling, precision, and proper heat control to create strong and reliable connections. Soldering is commonly used in electronics and craft projects to form stable electrical connections and assemble small wire structures.

Welding

Welding is the process of joining metal pieces together by applying high heat to melt the edges of the materials. The image illustrates how a welding tool produces intense heat that fuses the metal parts into a single, strong joint. This technique requires proper skill, safety precautions, and precise control to ensure a durable connection. Welding is commonly used in construction, manufacturing, and repair work to build or fix metal structures such as frames, machinery, and structural components.
