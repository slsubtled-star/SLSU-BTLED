Eye expression

Eye expression drawing is the process of practicing how to illustrate eyes in a way that clearly shows different emotions. The image demonstrates how students focus on the details of the eyes, such as shape, lines, and shading, to express feelings like happiness, sadness, or surprise. This activity helps develop observation skills, precision, and artistic creativity. Eye expression drawing is important in art and animation because it helps convey emotions and bring characters to life.

Miniature

This is the process of students making a model of a farm in the Agrifishery subject to understand agricultural systems. The activity involves constructing a miniature layout using materials like clay, cardboard, sticks, and toy animals to represent crops, livestock, irrigation, fish ponds, and farm structures. This technique is commonly used in vocational classes to teach sustainable farming practices, crop rotation, and integrated agri-fishery techniques for real-world food production.

Pictorial drawing

Pictorial drawing is the process of creating illustrations that represent three-dimensional objects in a realistic and visual manner. The image demonstrates how students draw objects in a way that shows depth, shape, and proportion on a flat surface. This activity helps learners understand how objects appear in real life and how they can be represented clearly through drawing. Pictorial drawings are commonly used in design, technical work, and visual presentations to communicate ideas effectively.

Storyboard

Storyboard creation is the process of students developing a series of drawings to visually organize and sequence a story. The illustration shows how each frame represents a scene or key moment in a project. This activity helps students plan the flow of events, ideas, and transitions before producing videos or animations. Creating a storyboard encourages creativity, organization, and clear visualization of the final project.
