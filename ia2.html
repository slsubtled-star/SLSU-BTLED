Industrial Arts - TEACHING

An Industrial Arts teaching demonstration is the process of instructing students on practical mechanical skills through guided, hands-on activities. The image illustrates an instructor demonstrating tasks such as gearbox disassembly while explaining the functions of parts like gears, shafts, and other transmission components. Students observe and practice using proper hand tools to separate and inspect the parts, check for wear, and understand the steps for reassembly. This method is commonly used in vocational classrooms to promote safe work practices, strengthen technical knowledge, and prepare learners for real-world repair and maintenance tasks.
