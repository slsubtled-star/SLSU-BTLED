Art creation

Art creation through painting and storyboard development is the process of students expressing ideas and telling visual stories using drawings and color. The image illustrates how learners sketch rough layouts, apply colors with brushes or markers on paper or canvas, and arrange panels in sequence to form a narrative. This activity helps students organize their concepts and visualize scenes for projects such as comics or animations. It is commonly used in art classes and creative projects to develop imagination, design skills, and visual storytelling for school presentations or media production.

Performance activities

Performance activities such as folk dancing and fashion show preparation involve students expressing creativity while developing physical coordination and confidence. The image illustrates learners practicing rhythmic dance steps, formations, and traditional movements to music as part of a folk dance routine. At the same time, students prepare to model outfits on a runway, demonstrating design concepts and presentation skills. These activities are commonly used in physical education classes and school programs to promote physical fitness, cultural appreciation, creativity, teamwork, and self-confidence.

Typing practice

Typing practice is the process of students completing a simple typing test to improve their keyboard skills and typing speed. The image illustrates learners typing words, sentences, or short passages on a computer within a set time limit. During the activity, their words per minute (WPM) and accuracy are measured to track progress and develop proficiency. Typing practice is commonly used in computer classes and school activities to strengthen digital literacy, office skills, and efficient data entry for everyday tasks.
