Teaching Demonstration

A teaching demonstration is the process of presenting a sample lesson to show how a topic can be taught effectively in the classroom. The image illustrates college students majoring in BTLEd (Bachelor of Technology and Livelihood Education) conducting a demonstration activity as part of their training. This activity allows future teachers to practice lesson delivery, classroom management, and instructional strategies. Teaching demonstrations help develop confidence, communication skills, and professional teaching competence.
